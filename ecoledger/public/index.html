How it works

  1. 1
    ActRide an electric bus, separate your waste, or go solar.
  2. 2
    VerifySigned tickets, a collector's review, or an admin's approval confirm it.
  3. 3
    Earn on AlgorandPoints are recorded on the Algorand blockchain as GRN tokens.
  4. 4
    RedeemTurn points into a discount code for transit, water or electricity.

EcoLedger is a prototype. Points have no cash value outside the municipal programme.
